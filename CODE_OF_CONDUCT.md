# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenForge is a community for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives. We are committed to making participation welcoming, respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, respectful and constructive.
- Welcome beginners and explain feedback clearly.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give credit to other people's work.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sharing spam, malicious content or deliberately misleading information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional, collaborative learning environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainers privately through the contact options on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Please include relevant links or context where possible.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. The maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or take other action appropriate to the situation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in OpenForge repository spaces, issues, pull requests and community spaces when someone is representing the project.
